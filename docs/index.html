FAQ

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 and Python 3.12. Node.js is only needed if you want to build the app yourself.

Is it really free?

Yes. The software is free and MIT licensed, and it runs on free AI models using your own free keys.

How fast is it?

Speed depends on the free models available at the time. Adding more keys helps.

Is this an official Nous Research product?

No. Custom Hermes is an independent customization of the open-source Hermes Agent by Nous Research, and it runs on your own computer.

Where does my data go?

Everything runs locally and binds to 127.0.0.1. Prompts go only to the model providers you configure.
